Reading the badges

BadgeMeaning
VerifiedKey info like price and hours read directly on the venue's official page.
Partly verifiedOnly the address or some items confirmed. Unconfirmed values stay "Pending."
OutdatedLast checked more than 90 days ago. Check with the venue before you go.
PendingNot yet read on an official page. We never fill it in by guessing.
Guests only · Members only · Men onlyWho can use it, as stated on the official page. For saunas, check this first.
CrossFit affiliate verified
HYROX official gym list verified		Only for places we saw ourselves in that official list on the date shown. No mark doesn't mean unregistered. It means still checking.

Check dates and sources

Every detail page ends with a last checked date and source links. Anything a venue told us directly is marked "venue reply" with a date. Prices and closing days change, so check the official page or the venue once more before you go.

Before you go

  • HANSET takes no bookings or payments. Sign up on each venue's or organizer's official page.
  • Saunas and cold plunges can be risky depending on your condition. With cardiovascular disease, during pregnancy, after drinking, or when you feel unwell, avoid them or consult a doctor. Nothing on this site is medical advice.
