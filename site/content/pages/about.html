About HANSET

Train. Sweat. One set. HANSET (한셋) puts CrossFit, HYROX, and hotel and boutique saunas in one wellness magazine.

What we do

An editorial team that loves CrossFit, HYROX and saunas. We gather drop-in boxes, hotel saunas, new boutique saunas and race dates in one place, and share the same facts as Instagram card news and short videos.

How we curate

  • Official sources only. We publish only what we read on a venue's or organizer's official page. Every entry carries a check date and a source link.
  • Not sure? "Pending." We never guess. Unconfirmed values stay "Pending." Prices seen only in third-party posts never go up.
  • Old info is flagged. Entries not checked in 90 days get an "Outdated" badge.
  • Our editorial calls aren't for sale. Prices, closing days and check dates are verified the same way for every venue, from official sources.
  • No copying other sites' databases, user reviews or photos. Simple facts like names, addresses and prices are confirmed on a venue's official channels, then rewritten in our own words.

Scope

Saunas center on hotel saunas and boutique saunas known through channels like Instagram. Large spas and hot springs with saunas, jjimjil rooms and open-air baths are kept separate, labeled "Large Spa · Hot Spring." Neighborhood bathhouses aren't covered. For now: Seoul, Busan and Jeju, plus some of Goyang (Gyeonggi). Verified places first, one at a time.

Something's off?

Prices and closing days change. Check with the venue once more before you go. Spot an error? Tell us via Corrections. How to read the site is in the Guide; common questions are in the FAQ.

HANSET doesn't give medical advice. We make no health claims, and sauna and cold-plunge safety notes sit with each entry.
