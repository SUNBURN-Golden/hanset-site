FAQ

What is HANSET?

A wellness magazine that lists CrossFit and HYROX boxes and hotel and boutique saunas, each with a check date and source. Our standards are on the About page.

How do you verify prices and hours?

We check each published price and time against an official source and show its link and check date.

Values from venue replies stay pending until an official page confirms them.

What does "Pending" mean?

A value we haven't read on an official page yet. We don't guess or copy it from elsewhere. It stays "Pending."

What's the "Outdated" badge?

Prices and times stay visible with this badge once their evidence is more than 90 days old. Check with the venue before you go.

What do "CrossFit affiliate verified" and "HYROX official gym list verified" mean?

They appear only on places we saw ourselves, on the date shown, in CrossFit's official affiliate pages or HYROX's official gym list. No mark means we didn't find it or are still checking. It doesn't mean the place isn't registered.

The place I want isn't listed.

We add places as we verify them on official pages. Unverified places don't go up. To suggest one, send the official page link through Corrections.

Can non-guests use hotel saunas?

It depends on the hotel. Some are guests only, some members and guests only, some open to non-guests, and some don't say. Check "Who can use" and "Not yet verified" on each detail page first.

Can I book or pay through HANSET?

No. HANSET takes no bookings or payments. Use the official site or booking links on each detail page.

Any precautions for the sauna?

With cardiovascular disease, during pregnancy, after drinking, or when you feel unwell, avoid saunas and cold plunges or consult a doctor. Nothing on this site is medical advice.

Info is wrong, or I run the venue and want it changed or removed. What do I do?

Email hanset@soulbound.co.kr as described on the Corrections page. We check the evidence, update it and change the check date. Edit and removal requests work the same way.

Do you collect personal data?

You can browse HANSET without an account. If you choose to join with Google, we store your email, your name if Google provides it, your Google account ID, your newsletter choice, and your sign-up date. You can join without receiving the weekly picks. We don’t use analytics or tracking scripts. Read our Privacy notice for details.

Do you publish venue photos?

No. We show venues only through hand-drawn illustrations.
