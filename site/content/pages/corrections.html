Corrections

Effective: 2026-10-18

See something wrong? Tell us. Venues and organizers are welcome to write directly.

What to send

  • The page URL and what needs fixing
  • Evidence if you can, like an official page link
  • If you represent the venue, say so (requests to edit or remove a listing work the same way)

What happens next

We check the evidence, update the entry and change its check date. Anything we can't confirm is marked "Pending."

Contact

Send corrections to hanset@soulbound.co.kr.
